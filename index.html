About

Hello! I am a Machine Learning Research Engineer at the University of Cambridge and I mainly work with Prof. Hiranya Peiris on applying machine learning to problems in astrophysics and cosmology, I am split between the IoA and the Zettascale Lab. I'm also involved with Miles Cranmer and the Polymathic AI collaboration on a part-time basis.

Part of my role involves providing HPC (high-performance computing) and RSE (research software engineering) training to incoming students at the IoA.

Previously, I was at the University of Oxford, working for the SKA Observatory and on the SPOTLIGHT project, focusing on scientific and high-performance computing for radio astronomy.

Interests

I am interested in using machine learning methods for scientific discovery and improving upon these methods to make them more energy efficient.

Within astrophysics and cosmology, our group is interested in galaxy formation and evolution and how it informs cosmology.

Outside of work, you'll find me being terrible at various sports: Footy, Ultimate Frisbee, Bouldering, Tennis/Padel, Chess(?), etc.
