About

Hello! I am a Machine Learning Research Engineer at the University of Cambridge and I mainly work with Prof. Hiranya Peiris on applying machine learning to problems in astrophysics and cosmology. I am split between the IoA and the Zettascale Lab. I'm also sometimes involved with Miles Cranmer and the Polymathic AI collaboration.

Part of my role involves providing HPC (high-performance computing) and RSE (research software engineering) training to incoming students at the IoA.

Previously, I was at the University of Oxford, working for the SKA Observatory and on the SPOTLIGHT project focusing on scientific and high-performance computing for radio astronomy.
